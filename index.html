Quick help

Talk or type in simple words. Tap an example to try it. Examples with a name go into the text box so you can change the name.

  • See everything savedAlso works as "friends list", "what is saved" or "show my birthdays".
  • Save a dateBirthdays, anniversaries, weddings or any date. The year is optional.
  • Save your own dateSay "my" or "our" instead of a name.
  • Find a dateAsk about one person.
  • TodaySee what is special today.
  • Coming upAdd "this week" or "this month" to narrow it.
  • Only one kindBirthdays, anniversaries, weddings and more.
  • Remove a dateDeletes it on this device.
  • Talk to meTap the mic and speak. Replies are read aloud. Tap Voice to turn that off.
